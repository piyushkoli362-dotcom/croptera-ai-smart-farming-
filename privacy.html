Privacy Notice

How interest and collaboration information is handled.

In the local preview, a submitted form is saved in a private Excel workbook on this computer. On the public website, forms remain disabled until the private Google Sheet connection is configured and verified.

Information collected

The farmer form collects name, email, optional state, district and user category, and consent. The talent form collects name, email, interest area, skills, links, motivation, availability, engagement preference and consent. The partnership form collects name, work email, organization, collaboration area, message and consent. A submission ID and time are stored with each completed entry.

Why it is collected

Farmer details are used for requested launch updates. Talent details are used to consider future collaboration interest. Partnership details are used to respond to enquiries. These are separate purposes. Registering interest does not guarantee product access, a role or a partnership.

Where it is stored

On the local preview, submitted entries are saved in a private local Excel workbook and a record file under the private outputs folder. These files are excluded from the public site build and Git. For the public website, submissions are sent through the website server to a private Google Sheet owned by Croptera AI. Only people granted access to that Google Sheet can view the entries.

Retention and deletion

Information should be kept only as long as needed for its purpose. To request access or deletion, contact Piyush Koli through the LinkedIn profile below and state which form and email were used. The project owner can remove a local record by submission ID and regenerate the workbook. For public entries, the owner can locate the entry in the appropriate private Google Sheet tab by email or submission ID and remove it after verifying the request.

Other data

The site does not use analytics or advertising scripts and does not ask for passwords, government identity numbers or financial information. Hosting providers may process standard server logs to serve the site.
